## 地点与年代

- 地图点 广州沙湾 · Shawan, Guangzhou · [113.32, 22.9]
- 地点角色 何氏创作与工尺谱发现地 · Centre of the He family’s music and place where the score was found
- 坐标为地图定位参考。[OpenStreetMap 定位](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=22.9&mlon=113.32#map=12/22.9/113.32)
- 时间与事件 20世纪二三十年代，沙湾何氏广东音乐广泛传播。现存《赛龙夺锦》手稿于2004年发现。 · The He family’s Shawan repertory circulated widely in the 1920s and 1930s. The surviving manuscript was found in 2004.
- 地点和音乐的关系 何柳堂据沙湾龙舟音乐材料反复整理《赛龙夺锦》。所选为珠海民族管弦乐团的演出，能听到龙舟鼓点如何进入乐队织体。
- Place and music · He Liutang developed the piece from Shawan dragon-boat music. The Zhuhai Chinese Orchestra version shows how boat-race rhythms enter an orchestral texture.
