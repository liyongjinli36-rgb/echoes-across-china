## 中英卡片与核验

李遇春与周天佑途中相逢，各自讲起家事。在写到韩城、杏花村的两句里，李遇春把地名唱得格外分明。

Li Yuchun and Zhou Tianyou meet on the road and exchange family histories. In Li’s first two lines, the names of Hancheng and Xinghua Village stand out as he sings them.

听前问题 · 唱到“韩城县”和“有家园”时，哪一个字拖得长？伴奏在哪个字后接上来？  At the ends of the lines naming Hancheng and the family home, which syllable lasts longest? Where does the accompaniment answer?

先跟李遇春报家门时的字音，再听苦音二六怎样在一句的末尾给尾字留出空间。 Follow the syllables as Li identifies his hometown, then listen for the room left at each line ending by the kuyin erliu tune pattern.
