- 地点角色 片段中海菜腔的传承区域 · Region associated with Haicaiqiang in the clip
- 坐标为地图定位参考。[OpenStreetMap 定位](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=23.71&mlon=102.49#map=12/23.71/102.49)

- Place and music · Shiping is a place of transmission for Yi Haicaiqiang. The clip combines it with dage, which also has a tradition in Weishan. The map locates the Haicaiqiang portion.
