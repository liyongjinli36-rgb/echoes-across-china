## 中英卡片与核验

Nawa is one of the Twelve Muqam. Musicians in Huangdi, Shache, have performed its prelude in the village. In this stage excerpt by the Tianshan Snow Lotus Muqam Ensemble, follow a line of the woman’s singing, then pick out the plucked strings and frame drums alongside her.
