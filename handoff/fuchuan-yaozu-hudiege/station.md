- Place and music · Yao butterfly songs circulate in Fuchuan and neighbouring Yao communities. The form takes its name from repeated butterfly-related vocables. The selected performance is by the county cultural centre’s singers.

## 中英卡片与核验

- Listening question · Find a line sung in two parts. Which part changes pitch first, and what does the other do at that moment?
- 听法 以一句的起音为参照，辨认两条声部何时分开，何时重新落在一起。
