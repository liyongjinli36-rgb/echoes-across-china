## 地点与年代

- 地图点 甘孜康定 · Kangding, Garzê · [101.96, 30.05]
- 地点角色 溜溜调流传与整理地 · Place of circulation and arrangement of the Liuliu tune
- 坐标为地图定位参考。[OpenStreetMap 定位](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=30.05&mlon=101.96#map=12/30.05/101.96)
- 时间与事件 20世纪三四十年代在康定城乡传唱，后来整理为《康定情歌》。 · Widely sung around Kangding in the 1930s and 1940s and later arranged as Kangding Love Song.
- 地点和音乐的关系 《康定情歌》与康定城北、雅拉沟一带传唱的溜溜调有联系。20世纪三四十年代，该调已在康定城乡传唱，后来经整理以现名传播。
- Place and music · Kangding Love Song is related to the Liuliu tune sung north of Kangding and in the Yala valley. It circulated locally in the 1930s and 1940s and was later arranged under its current title.
